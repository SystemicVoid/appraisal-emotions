# Sofroniew arms: results and deep read

*2026-08-17. Covers the two E0 arms landed on `main` at `49cff76`, their verification against the `rental-2026-08-16` release, and the E1 + E2 readouts run on this host today against those bases (`map_geometry_report{,_valence_only}.json` and `expectation_control_report.json` in each arm's `emotions/` directory — versioned CLI outputs, reproducible from the checkout). Vector geometry marked "computed" comes from session scripts over the digest-verified payloads and is not yet behind a CLI entry point.*

## What matters most

The Sofroniew arms were built to answer one reviewer question: **is the appraisal-to-emotion result a property of emotion-concept geometry, or of our story prompt?** With E1 and E2 now scored on both arms, the answer splits cleanly by readout:

1. **The E2 comparison signature is not a property of our prompt.** On both paper-prompt bases, the emotion-probe readout at the reveal token tracks the *stated expectation* with the outcome text held fixed (reward-matched cells), and the *realised outcome* with expectation held fixed (EV-matched cells), sign-congruent on both axes with every permutation p at the 1/10,001 floor. That is the full conjunction (`comparison_signature.holds = true` on both axes, both arms) — the same qualitative result as the base basis, now shown on a basis extracted under Sofroniew et al. 2026's own recipe. Slopes are about half the base basis's size (pooled within-cell 0.010–0.016 vs 0.020–0.029), consistent with these bases' lower per-word precision.
2. **The E1 positive-pole inheritance contrast replicates in direction but not in floor-clearance.** The outcome-vs-control family contrast on the positive pole is positive on every surface tried so far — wide project-prompt +0.0346 (perm p = 0.018, clears both floors), sofroniew raw +0.0195 (p = 0.048), sofroniew projected +0.0229 (p = 0.020) — but on the paper-prompt arms the max contrast sits *below* the pre-registered label-shuffle p95 floor (raw 0.0195 vs 0.0241; projected 0.0229 vs 0.0230, a miss of 0.0001 at block 32 and 0.0034 at block 35). Per the recorded reading rule this records **pilot-suggestive, floor-not-cleared** on these arms: below-floor contrasts are `harness_inadequate` for effects of that size, not evidence of absence — and with no reliability capture for these arms, no MDE exists to price them against.
3. **The rest of the E1 panel is consistent across all surfaces, including its failures.** P1 holds everywhere (rho of cos(v_RPE, e_j) against valence: +0.78 to +0.83). The negative-pole contrast is null everywhere (−0.006 to +0.004). The three-level outcome ordering fails *the same way* everywhere — `outcome_confirm` carries the largest positive residual on every surface (means 0.031–0.043 vs outcome_pos 0.012–0.022), an unanticipated but stable pattern the recorded-expectations policy says to report. P4's premise holds everywhere (`v_absrpe` loads the arousal PC, |cos| 0.03–0.07, vs ≤0.012 on the valence PC), but its surprise-vs-arousal-matched contrast clears only on wide (+0.016, p = 0.024; projected +0.010, p = 0.06–0.11; raw −0.004, p = 0.77). P5a passes on both arms (`sad` sits inside the residual spread, rank 10/111).
4. **One control failure to carry:** the raw arm fails P5c on `v_absrpe` (style-vector residual −0.064 against a 0.046 band, both blocks, both readouts), so the raw arm's `v_absrpe` readouts are capped by their own scale control. The projected arm passes P5c on all three families, as does wide.

## What landed

Both directories are **E0 artifacts** (design §4 "E0 — Emotion basis + sensitivity gate"): emotion-concept vectors for the 111-word set plus the `style_control` pseudo-label, at every block of `Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B` (revision `6a9e13bd`, `model_key qwen_30b_primary`), under the paper's own story prompt (`story_recipe: "sofroniew"`, arXiv 2604.07729). The projected arm additionally applies the paper's neutral-dialogue confound projection (`variance_target 0.5`). Payload shape is (112, 64, 5120) float64 in both arms, no NaN/inf, no zero or duplicate rows, grand-mean centring exact to 2.6e-08 relative. Everything that should match across arms is byte-identical: `stimulus_hash`, `seed 7`, `words_file_sha256` (which matches `data/emotion_words.json` on disk and the norms manifest), model revision, label order, and the full `recipe_manifest`.

The pass is robust, not knife-edge: both arms plateau at 0.81–0.85 across the entire second half of the stack (mean 0.828/0.836 over blocks 20–45), and the G0 value was reproduced to six decimals from the released npz payloads — the arrays on the release are the arrays the gate was computed on. It also survives swapping the operationalization: against graded NRC-VAD valence instead of the minted ±1 labels, PC1 reads 0.837/0.853, and excluding the 8 zero-coded `surprise` words gives 0.859/0.858. A label-shuffle floor on G0 itself (2,000 valence permutations, computed) has p95 = 0.185 — the observed value sits more than twice the largest shuffle. Two caveats travel with the headline number: the selected block is the argmax of the gated statistic, so 0.8407/0.8498 are optimistic by selection (the flat plateau bounds the exposure); and G0 as implemented is only the rho clause of design §4's conjunction — the "per-word probe sanity on a held-out story sample" clause is unimplemented everywhere (this equally affects the earlier `base` and `wide` runs). Reading the basis at the reveal directions' block 35 instead of 32 costs almost nothing (0.8401/0.8434), so the {32, 35} headline-block split in the E1 reports is not a sensitivity problem.

## How the E1/E2 runs were wired

Both arms were scored with the repo's own CLI against the 27B reveal directions already on disk (`runs/reveal_rpe_base/reveal_rpe/reveal_directions.json`, `source_verdict "separable-signed-rpe"`, block 35), mirroring `just map-geometry-wide`: the primary report partials valence *and* arousal (NRC-VAD numeric norms, 111/111 coverage), the companion report valence only, 10,000 permutations, 1,000 null draws, seed 7. `residualize_on: [valence, arousal]` was added to both sofroniew configs before any map-geometry report existed for these arms — wiring the widened design's pre-registered primary, not amending a readout that had met data. E2 (`expectation-control`) projects the certified battery's reveal-token states onto each arm's PC1 and `elated`−`disappointed` axes and regresses on signed RPE within reward-matched cells (60 cells, 960 rows) and EV-matched cells (124 cells, 1,984 rows), cluster-aware permutation null. The valence-only E1 companion tells the same story as the primary throughout (positive pole +0.0115–0.0253, floors not cleared).

## What the projection actually did

The projected arm's own control tables settle this cleanly, and the answer is: **almost nothing where the gate is read**. At block 32: before projection 0.84845, after 0.84975 (+0.0013), random same-width orthonormal frame 0.84845 (exactly the unprojected value). The projection's real effect is confined to the early stack — +0.42 at block 1, +0.33 at block 0, mean +0.13 over blocks 0–8, then under +0.01 essentially everywhere from block 9 on — where the neutral subspace evidently absorbs a generic early-layer direction that suppresses valence structure. The random-frame null behaves exactly as a floor should (mean |Δrho| 0.00012 across blocks), so the early-block lift is specific to the neutral subspace; it just doesn't matter by mid-stack.

This kills one tempting reading of the headline pair: **0.841 → 0.850 is not a projection effect.** The two arms are fully independent generation draws — 0 of 1,992 matched `(emotion, topic, story_index)` keys share any text, because `seed 7` is the permutation/bootstrap seed and never reaches the sampler at temperature 1.0. The raw arm vs the projected arm's own before-projection value (0.8407 vs 0.8485, Δ = 0.0078) is the run-to-run corpus-resampling scale, roughly 6× the projection's +0.0013. This is also the in-repo confirmation of the paper's own footnote that its qualitative findings hold without the projection. The same caution applies to every cross-arm E1/E2 difference above (including the raw arm's P5c failure where the projected arm passes): arm differences confound the projection with a fresh generation draw.

## Geometry of the basis (computed, block 32, 111 word rows)

The structure the design needs is present, at large effect sizes, in both arms:

- **Bipolar valence structure dominates.** Mean cosine within the positive pole +0.30, within the negative pole +0.27, across poles −0.29 (projected: +0.32 / +0.28 / −0.31). Within-pole minus between-pole mean cosine is +0.57 at Cohen d ≈ 3.0 (projected +0.61, d ≈ 3.1). The most anti-aligned pairs bottom out near −0.66, so the space is a bipolar cone, not a one-dimensional axis; the tightest pairs are synonym clusters (`delighted`~`overjoyed` +0.83; `peaceful`~`tranquil` +0.87 projected).
- **PC1 is the valence axis; PC2 reads as arousal.** PC1 explains 30.5%/31.3% of variance and reproduces G0 exactly; it is nearly arousal-free (|rho| vs NRC-VAD arousal 0.03/0.05). PC2 (12.1%/13.2%) tracks arousal at |rho| = 0.81/0.82 while nearly valence-free, and the high-arousal families separate on it at d ≈ −1.2, permutation p < 1e-4. Both axes P4 needs therefore exist and are separable — and the E1 reports confirm `v_absrpe` loads PC2, not PC1, on every surface. PC3 does not replicate across arms (score |r| = 0.63, sign flip on the family separation) — nothing should be claimed past PC2.
- **Cross-arm stability lives at the axis level, not the pair level.** PC1 agrees across arms at |r| = 0.98, PC2 at 0.91 — but individual pairwise cosines carry an implied single-run sd of ~0.10 against a between-pair sd of 0.34. This is why the E1 named pairs (all |statistic| ≤ 0.07, all p ≥ 0.14, signs mixed across arms) are read as noise around the family statistics, not as five independent tests: a single word-pair cosine is not a reproducible object at this k.
- **One wrinkle for P4's siting:** the `surprise` family is coded valence 0 but is not geometrically neutral — mean cosine +0.12 with the negative pole, −0.18 with the positive, mean PC1 z ≈ +0.5 toward the negative side. Excluding it strengthens rather than weakens G0, but P4's premise that `v_absrpe` picks this family out "without loading a valence pole" meets a family that already leans negative in concept space — one plausible contributor to the P4 contrast clearing only on the wide surface.
- **Cross-family cosines preview why E1 is a residual readout:** same-pole outcome and non-outcome families sit at +0.25 to +0.32 — valence-matched controls are not separated from the disconfirmation families by raw cosine, which is exactly why the P1/P2 readouts partial valence out first.

## Corpus quality

Filters reconcile exactly (raw: 2,161 generated, 2,026 kept, drops = 125 `too_short` + 10 `names_target`, rate 0.0625; projected: 2,164 / 2,038 / 126 `too_short`, 0.0582), the first-contact checkpoints passed at 0.10 against a 0.50 ceiling, and there are zero refusals, zero separator leakage, zero empty texts. Four findings qualify the corpus, ranked:

1. **Call-level truncation touches ~23% of the kept corpus.** The 700-token budget is spent per *call* (3–6 stories per call against a configured 2), so 95%+ of calls exhaust it, every `too_short` drop is the last piece of its call, and 469/2,026 (raw) and 464/2,038 (projected) *kept* stories end mid-sentence. Mean pooling weights those unresolved tails equally with complete stories.
2. **The run over-delivered 60% and unevenly.** `stories_per_emotion: 12` describes the request, not the artifact: the `<NEW STORY>` splitter recovered ~19.3 pieces per label (kept 14–21), and `_yield_check` only raises on shortfall. Realized k ≈ 18 is the case `docs/design/e1-widening.md` bootstrapped at a 1-in-4 to 1-in-5 chance of re-measuring ICC < 0.7 on the primary readout — directly relevant to why the E1 floors were missed here at contrasts the wide run (k = 24) cleared.
3. **Six topics, all negative or ambivalent premises, with high cross-label stereotypy.** All 672 (label × topic) cells are filled and token mass is balanced within 5%, but positive-valence labels carry negated-affect constructions at 42.5% vs 27.0% for negative labels (writing `elated` about a plagiarism premise forces "it wasn't anger…"), so positive concepts and affect-negation syntax are partly confounded. 21% of rows share a 40-character opening with another row; the largest group is 24 *different* labels writing near-parallel openings on one topic, with cross-label same-topic Jaccard (0.156) exceeding within-call same-label Jaccard (0.086). Effective independent n per label is below the nominal 18.
4. **An unexplained draw asymmetry:** `names_target` fired 10 times in the raw arm and 0 in the projected (Fisher p = 9.6e-4), re-confirmed by re-running the repo's own classifier over every row. Nothing in the recorded configuration accounts for it; draw-level noise, not a projection effect.

The neutral corpus (projected arm) is fit for its role but thin: 22 of 24 configured transcripts (the splitter silently lost 2 — `drop_rate: 0.0` is computed against pieces *obtained*, hiding an 8.3% shortfall), rank-21 covariance in 5,120 dimensions, 1–6 components per block. Several transcripts open with un-tagged instruction lines or are dominated by code blocks. Since the projection barely moves the gate, this doesn't threaten the result, but the artifact should not be read as evidence the confound subspace was well estimated.

## What this licenses

Per the reports' own `verdict_cap`, everything here is bounded at **present-and-separable, pilot-suggestive**, on this model/surface/recipe only. The strongest composite sentence these artifacts support: *on Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B, emotion-concept bases extracted under Sofroniew et al. 2026's own story recipe pass the E0 sensitivity gate in both arms (0.841 / 0.850 vs 0.60); on those bases, the reveal-token emotion-probe readout reproduces the E2 comparison signature — tracking stated expectation with outcome fixed and realised outcome with expectation fixed, sign-congruent on both axes at the permutation floor — and the E1 positive-pole valence-residual contrast is positive with permutation p ≤ 0.05 but below the label-shuffle floor these arms' own harness sets, so its size on this surface remains an open question the current sample cannot price.* Not licensed: any appraisal-inheritance claim at floor-clearing strength from these arms alone; any functional-use claim (E3 territory); any reading of a below-floor contrast as absence; and — throughout — emotion words are concept labels, never state attributions. No welfare, sentience, or experience claim in either direction.

## What remains

- **Reliability capture (GPU, gated on approval):** ~2,000 read-only forwards per arm would give the ICC/MDE term that would price the below-floor E1 contrasts — the exact gap that made the first E1 null unreadable. Without new GPU time this stays open, and the E1 verdict on these arms stays at pilot-suggestive.
- **Rails debt (off-GPU, cheap):** the paper-prompt surface was never reality-sampled (the 2026-08-14 record covers the project prompt); the in-run first-contact checkpoints passed and this analysis read all the landed samples, but no post-run read record exists in `results/`.
- The four `map_geometry` reports and two `expectation_control` reports, plus the two config edits (`residualize_on`), are on the working tree and not yet committed.

## Findings worth filing

- `filter_freeze_status` is a hardcoded `"BLIND"` literal (`emotion_vectors.py:1117`) in all four 27B emotion runs, despite a `Literal["BLIND", "reality-sampled"]` type — the field carries no information.
- `_yield_check` (`emotion_vectors.py:342`) is one-sided; a 60% splitter over-delivery passes silently and the recorded `stories_per_emotion` no longer describes the artifact.
- Neutral-corpus `drop_rate` is computed against pieces obtained, not configured, so the `<NEW DIALOGUE>` splitter's 22/24 loss reads as 0.0 — a blind freeze whose only observability is this file.
- `config.py` default `max_tokens = 320` vs the executed 700; the committed default no longer documents the run, and the 700-per-call budget is what drives the truncation finding.
- G0's held-out per-word probe clause (design §4 E0) is unimplemented in `src/` — the recorded pass is the rho clause alone, in all runs.
- Doc drift, already tracked in `e1-widening.md` §7 but now load-bearing: `experiment.md` §5 still says "84 words" and `sofroniew-recipe.md` §2/§4 still promise "the same 84-word set" and a one-variable A/B that no landed pair of runs actually forms (base differs in prompt *and* width; wide differs in prompt *and* stories-per-word; the E1 comparison above is therefore prompt+k, not prompt alone).
- `min_token = 50` doubles as drop threshold and capture-window offset, so a story with 51 tokens contributes one token to its label mean (~20 stories per arm contribute ≤10 tokens).
