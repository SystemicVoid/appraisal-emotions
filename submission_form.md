## Project Details

**Project Title\***
> [DRAFT — pick one or edit] Appraisal Geometry: Does Reward Prediction Error Shape Emotion-Concept Representations in Qwen3.6-27B?

**Project Summary\***
> Reward prediction error (RPE) — the difference between received and expected reward — predicts momentary well-being in humans (Rutledge et al., 2014). We test whether an analogous computation is represented and read out in a large language model, Qwen3.6-27B. On 1,984 affect-neutral described gambles, a signed-RPE direction is strongly separable in the residual stream (AUROC 0.985). Emotion-concept readouts built independently from generated stories track the reward-versus-expectation comparison itself — reward and expectation contribute with opposite sign and comparable magnitude (ratios 1.11 and 1.25) — the same signature Rutledge et al. use to identify RPE in humans. Behaviourally, positive versus negative prior outcomes shift the model +0.19 logits toward subsequent risk-taking (p ≈ 10^-4), consistent with the house-money effect. Interventions establish that the signal is carried, but not yet that it is causally used. Recent outcome history may be an internal state variable worth monitoring in agentic systems.
